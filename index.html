BEFORE LIFTOFF

A few things to know.

What kind of game is this?

A space building and management game with idle and incremental progression. Plan your base, connect production and work toward research and exploration goals.

Is the game free?

The game is free to download and includes ads and optional in-app purchases. Google Play shows availability for your country and device.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. An internet connection is required to play and save your progress online.

Which languages does the game support?

The game currently supports English and Portuguese. This website is available in additional languages; those translations do not change the languages available inside the game.
