Privacy Policy

Space Explorer: Idle Tycoon

Last updated: October 17, 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Alan Michael, publishing as alanmikeo, is responsible for the personal data processed to operate Space Explorer: Idle Tycoon. For privacy questions or requests, contact alanmikeo@proton.me. This policy covers the game and communications about it; service providers also describe their own practices in the policies linked below.

2. Information used by the game

Age range. Before connecting, the game asks for an age range, with no preselected answer and an option not to disclose it. Only the range is kept locally on the device; no date of birth is requested or uploaded to the game database. The advertising SDK receives the corresponding age-treatment signals. An undisclosed age receives the protected treatment described below.

Account and sign-in. Firebase Authentication creates a unique player ID when an online game session begins. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase process account information such as your name, email address, provider identifier and authentication credentials. The game may display your email to identify the connected account. We do not receive your Google password.

Game progress. We store progress linked to your player ID, including your base and planetary buildings, inventory, research, discoveries, expeditions, tutorial progress, balances, upgrades, production and reward records. This is needed to save and recover the game and prevent duplicate deliveries. Gameplay requires an internet connection.

Purchases. Google Play and RevenueCat process product identifiers, transaction and purchase records, purchase status and acquired benefits. The game uses the Firebase player ID to associate purchases with the right player. We do not receive your full card number, bank credentials or Google password.

Advertising and technical data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process an IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, advertising or app identifiers where permitted, app/ad interactions and technical performance information. The data used depends on the applicable privacy settings, consent, age treatment and provider configuration. Ad-related data can support ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention.

Support. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email, message and any information needed to investigate the request. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens or full payment details.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location for gameplay. It has no player chat, public player profiles or user-uploaded photos. Trading visitors are game characters.

3. Why information is processed

We use information to provide online access, save and recover progress, operate production and expeditions, deliver and restore purchases, verify rewarded ads, prevent abuse and duplicate rewards, diagnose failures and respond to support or privacy requests. Where legally applicable, processing relies on providing the service you request, consent, legal obligations or legitimate interests such as service security. Those interests do not replace consent or other authorizations required for children or advertising.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The game uses Google/Firebase for authentication and cloud storage, Google AdMob and its consent tools for ads and privacy choices, and Google Play and RevenueCat for purchases and entitlement validation. Support messages are received through Proton Mail. These providers process information for their respective services and may retain their own records.

We may disclose information where required by law or to investigate fraud, protect service security or respond to a valid rights request. We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising-related processing and applicable regional privacy choices are described separately; this statement does not mean that no information is sent to advertising providers.

Provider policies:

  • Google and Firebase: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Proton: https://proton.me/legal/privacy

5. Ads, purchases and choices

Players under 18 and players who do not disclose their age receive only occasional banners, with personalization disabled. The game applies child treatment to under-16 and undisclosed-age requests, teen treatment to ages 16–17, and a maximum G ad content rating. It uses Google Mobile Ads, a Families self-certified advertising SDK. These players are not offered rewarded videos, automatic fullscreen ads or real-money purchases in this version.

Adults may see rewarded ads, occasional banners and occasional interstitial ads, subject to applicable privacy choices and availability. Rewarded videos offer the stated benefit if the provider confirms the reward. The optional remove-ads purchase removes banners and automatic interstitials; rewarded videos remain optional. This Android build removes the advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions. Age and consent settings are applied before advertising initialization and ad requests.

Where a privacy-choice form is required and available, the game exposes Settings → Ad privacy. You can also review your device's advertising privacy settings. Declining consent can limit ad availability; buying remove-ads is not equivalent to deleting your account or purchase records. Purchases are optional and processed through Google Play.

6. Children and parents

The intended audience includes children and older players. Children may need help from a parent or guardian to understand this policy. Parents and guardians can contact alanmikeo@proton.me to ask about their child's data, request access or deletion, or raise a concern. We may need sufficient information to verify the requester's authority without requesting excessive information.

Under-16 and undisclosed-age players start with a Firebase-generated player ID without being offered Google sign-in. Google account linking and recovery are available from age 16; real-money purchases and the RevenueCat integration are available only to adults aged 18 or over. Existing saves and previously linked accounts are preserved, rather than erased by the age screen.

The game does not implement verifiable parental consent or unlock these restricted features with a simple parent confirmation. A parent can contact us for questions, correction of the saved age range, or a child's data request. The age range is a device preference, not a verified identity or proof of consent. Anonymous online progress still involves a persistent player identifier and technical service data; anonymous does not mean no data is processed.

7. Storage, security and international processing

The game keeps session credentials and preferences in its private app storage and stores progress in Firebase/Google Cloud. Purchase and entitlement records are also handled by RevenueCat and Google Play. Deleting the app alone does not delete cloud data or transaction records.

Connections to the game's Firebase services use HTTPS. Provider SDKs use encrypted network connections according to their documentation. Access to game-progress documents is restricted by authenticated player ID. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Applicable transfer safeguards and provider terms apply where required; this policy does not imply that every record stays in Brazil or on your device.

8. Retention

Account and progress data are kept while needed to provide and recover the service, unless deletion is requested or another applicable retention rule requires a different period. We may retain limited transaction, fraud, security or legally required records for as long as necessary for those purposes. Providers have their own retention periods. Any retained exceptions should be explained when a deletion request is handled; data should not be retained indefinitely just because a request was made.

9. Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or other information about your data, withdraw applicable consent, or raise a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority. Contact alanmikeo@proton.me with the subject Space Explorer — Privacy.

To request deletion of your game account and associated data, use the subject Space Explorer — Delete account. Include your player ID if available, or the email linked to your game account, and explain whether you want account deletion or a more limited data request. Parents can identify their request as being for their child. We will verify ownership or authority before taking an irreversible action. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens.

Deletion concerns the game account, saved progress and related data, including provider records we control, subject to necessary legal or security retention. It does not delete your Google account or itself request a Google Play refund. Requests are handled manually. In the app, use Settings → Request account and data deletion, or Privacy on the connection screen. You can open an email draft or copy the request, which includes your player ID when available. Nothing is sent automatically. The website also provides the same contact path.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when data practices change and show the effective date on the published version. Material changes requiring notice or consent will be handled as required. Contact alanmikeo@proton.me with any questions.
