Account & data deletion

Request deletion of your Space Explorer: Idle Tycoon account and associated data.

Contact Alan Michael (alanmikeo) at alanmikeo@proton.me with the subject Space Explorer — Delete account.

Write deletion request

What to include

Include your player ID if available, or the email linked to your game account. Say whether the request is for yourself or your child. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or full payment details.

What happens next

The developer handles requests manually and verifies ownership or parental authority before an irreversible deletion. Sending an email starts a request; it does not instantly delete data.

What is covered

The game account, cloud progress and associated data controlled by the developer, including records with relevant service providers. Limited records may need to be kept for legal, security or transaction obligations; the response should explain applicable exceptions.

This does not delete your Google account or automatically request a Google Play refund. Removing the app does not delete cloud records.
